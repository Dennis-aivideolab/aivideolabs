# Cookie Policy

**Last Updated: May 2026**

## 1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit our website. They allow websites to remember your preferences and improve your browsing experience.

## 2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

### 2.1 Essential Cookies
These are necessary for the website to function properly:
- Session management
- Security functions
- Load balancing

### 2.2 Analytics Cookies
These help us understand how you use our website:
- Google Analytics – to track page views, bounce rates, and user behavior
- These cookies are anonymous and do not identify you personally

### 2.3 Marketing Cookies
These allow us to show you relevant content:
- Retargeting pixels
- Conversion tracking

## 3. Cookie Consent

### 3.1 Consent Banner
When you first visit our website, a consent banner appears. You can:
- **Accept All** – Accept all cookies
- **Reject** – Reject non-essential cookies
- **Preferences** – Choose which cookies to allow

### 3.2 Changing Your Preferences
You can change your cookie preferences at any time by clicking "Cookie Settings" in the footer.

## 4. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to analyze website traffic. Google Analytics uses cookies to track:
- Pages visited
- Time spent on pages
- User location
- Device type

### 4.1 IP Anonymization
Your IP address is anonymized and not stored in full.

### 4.2 Data Sharing
Google Analytics data may be shared with Google for service improvement.

### 4.3 Opt-Out
You can opt-out of Google Analytics by:
- Installing the [Google Analytics Opt-Out Extension](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout)
- Rejecting analytics cookies in our consent banner

## 5. Third-Party Cookies

Our website may include content from third parties that use their own cookies:
- YouTube
- Instagram
- Other embedded content

These third parties have their own cookie policies.

## 6. Managing Cookies in Your Browser

You can control cookies through your browser settings:

**Chrome:** Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies and Other Site Data
**Firefox:** Preferences → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
**Safari:** Preferences → Privacy → Cookies and Website Data
**Edge:** Settings → Privacy → Cookies

You can:
- Delete all cookies
- Block cookies from specific websites
- Allow only essential cookies

**Note:** Blocking cookies may affect website functionality.
