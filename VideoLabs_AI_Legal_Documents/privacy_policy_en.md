## 4. Google Analytics

This website uses Google Analytics, a web analytics service of Google Inc. (Google).

### 4.1 Scope of Processing

Google Analytics uses cookies – text files that are stored on your computer and enable analysis of your website usage.

Information collected by Google Analytics is typically transmitted to Google servers in the USA and stored there.

We have configured Google Analytics to anonymize IP addresses (IP masking). This means your IP address is shortened by Google before storage.

- Disabling cookies in your browser
- Installing the Google Analytics opt-out browser extension
- Selecting "Reject Cookies" in our cookie banner

## 5. Cookies

### 5.1 What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device. They allow us to collect information about your usage and improve the website.

### 5.2 Types of Cookies

**Technically Necessary Cookies:** These are required for the website to function (e.g., session cookies). These cannot be disabled.

**Analytics Cookies:** These provide statistics about website usage (Google Analytics). You can reject these.

**Marketing Cookies:** These enable personalized advertising. You can reject these.

### 5.3 Cookie Banner

On your first visit, a cookie banner appears. You can choose which cookies you want to accept.
